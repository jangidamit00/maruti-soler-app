Solar information

Things worth knowing before you install.

3 types of solar plants
  • On-grid: connected to the electricity grid, no battery. Cheapest and most common for homes.
  • Off-grid: disconnected from the grid, works with a battery. More expensive.
  • Hybrid: both grid and battery. Keeps working during a power cut.
What is net metering?

During the day, any extra electricity your plant produces flows back into the grid and is deducted from your bill.

How does the subsidy work? (PM Surya Ghar)
  1. Register at pmsuryaghar.gov.in
  2. Get approval from your electricity provider.
  3. Get the plant installed by a registered installer.
  4. Subsidy is paid directly into your bank account.

Subsidy: fixed ₹78,000 for 3 kW to 10 kW. This scheme runs until March 2027.
